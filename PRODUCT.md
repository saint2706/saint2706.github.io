# Product brief

A personal website centered on Rishabh Agrawal, designed as an evolving personal universe rather than a narrow professional landing page. The experience should make visitors curious enough to explore.

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors, confirmed by the owner 2026-11-04: recruiters and hiring teams, and peers and collaborators (developers, data people, classmates). Learners and general readers may arrive but are not the first audience.

## Success

The owner's stated first-visit goal (2026-11-04): a visitor comes away with a sense of who Rishabh is beyond work, through the personal space. Judging technical work and reaching him remain supported paths (see Project storytelling and Professional/contact).

## Spaces

1. **Professional:** identity, selected projects, editorial case studies, verified professional details, relevant writing and contact.
2. **Personal:** selectively curated interests and cultural explorations, separate navigation and content-led visual personality.
3. **Laboratory:** curated prototypes/experiments, separate from the main professional presentation.

The professional/personal transition is deliberate. Do not publish every work-in-progress.

## Interests

Seven areas may appear when substantive: technology/programming/software engineering; data science/analytics/visualization; business/management/strategy; education/learning/teaching; art/paintings/mythology/visual culture; books/literature/reading; music/cultural interests. They are not a requirement to launch seven empty pages.

## Homepage

A distinctive visual opening composition should establish atmosphere before asking visitors to read. Identity, projects and interests are discovered progressively. Avoid a generic hero consisting only of heading, subtitle and CTA buttons.

## Project storytelling

Initial case-study candidates: Coding-For-MBA, Attendance Management System using Face Recognition, Programming-Challenges-5. Final sequence follows audit evidence and approval. Each full case study should explain context, problem, constraints, reasoning, process, technical decisions, implementation, challenges, outcomes, limitations and supporting evidence. Other entries may be lighter.

## Content

Support essays, articles, tutorials, documentation, notes, research and curated collections. Keep publishing low-maintenance and Git-friendly. AI may edit supplied material but must not invent personal voice or facts. Every factual claim needs evidence; personal experiences/opinions/achievements need approval. English launch; multilingual-ready architecture.

## Professional/contact

Contact paths: email, GitHub, LinkedIn and Instagram. Verify details before publication. Decide after audit whether professional profile/resume should be integrated, dedicated or downloadable.

## Principles/exclusions

- No user portrait/selfie/actual photo or AI likeness.
- No invented brand or mandatory conventional logo.
- No empty/thin sections, placeholders or fabricated metrics.
- No novelty chatbot/AI feature, no visitor analytics or unnecessary tracking.
- Prefer links/native previews over heavy embeds.
- No paid service/recurring cost without explicit approval.
- Success is originality, storytelling, authenticity, usability, accessibility, performance and maintainability.
