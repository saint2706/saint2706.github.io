# P00-T03 — Audit content, projects and asset provenance

**Phase:** P00  
**Status:** complete  
**Authorization:** pre-authorized-audit-only  
**Dependencies:** P00-T01

## Objective

Assess content, personal claims, candidate projects and image assets for preservation, verification and rights.

## Repository context

Resume/profile and project data are source material, not blanket approval to publish.

## Scope

- Inventory profile/resume/project/writing/feed content
- Audit flagship repos and evidence
- Inventory images/assets and their provenance
- Classify claims and personal approval needs

## Preconditions

- No content publishing or rewriting
- Separate repo facts from personal contribution claims
- Treat rights and privacy conservatively

## Ready-to-copy prompt

> Audit content and media without publishing changes. Examine profile/resume facts, project records, external links and all image assets. Review the candidate flagship repositories and report what evidence supports technical claims. Classify claims as repository-verified, externally-verifiable, user-confirmation-required, stale or unsupported. Capture license uncertainty and missing facts.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All content sources inventoried
- [ ] Claim categories and evidence recorded
- [ ] Asset provenance/license status recorded
- [ ] Flagship evidence gaps documented
- [ ] No invented claims or publication
