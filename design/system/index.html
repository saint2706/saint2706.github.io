Type

A 1.25 scale from 16px. Headings: Young Serif, a single-weight face drawn after old specimen and label lettering. Body and interface: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, designed by the Braille Institute so similar letters stay distinct. Code and data: Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono. All three are SIL Open Font Licence, self-hosted, about 76 KB in total.

Serif heading, sans body

Used for Work and Off the clock. The serif gives headings an archival feel and the sans keeps long text plain. Body lines stop at about 65 characters.

Sans throughout

Used for the Lab, where the pages are tools and notes. Monospace is used only for code, data and measurements, never as decoration.
