Measured contrast

Read from the colours on the page and recomputed whenever you switch palettes. AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text, focus rings and control borders. Rules are decorative separators, so they have no minimum.

Serif heading, sans body

Used for Work and Off the clock. The serif gives headings an archival feel and the sans keeps long text plain. Body lines stop at about 65 characters.

Sans throughout

Used for the Lab, where the pages are tools and notes. Monospace is used only for code, data and measurements, never as decoration.
